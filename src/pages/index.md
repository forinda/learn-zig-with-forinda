# Zero to Hero in Zig

<img class="hero-art" src="/art/agama-rider.svg" alt="Agama, the course's red-headed lizard, racing a road bike in a Zig-orange helmet" width="480" height="360" />

Learn to program in Zig from zero by building one game: **Terminal Tour**, a cycling race in your terminal.
You ride, rivals race you, a breakaway goes up the road, the bunch chases, and you sprint for the line.

```text
 TERMINAL TOUR · Compile Pass · 12.5 km                       km 9.8 / 12.5
 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Break   3 riders                     ▶▶▶
  Bunch  42 riders   +0:24      ▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶
  You    in the bunch          ▲
 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Power 285 W  ███████████░░░░   Energy ██████████████░░░   Speed 41 km/h
  9.6 km  The chase is on: Team Allocator pull at the front
  [↑↓] power  [space] sprint  [s] sit in  [q] quit
```

No programming experience is needed. The **Guide** teaches each idea and adds it to the game; the **Course**
checks you've got it, with quizzes, a snippet to predict, a bug to fix and the step to build yourself. Every step
of the game is a complete project you can run, so you can start anywhere and never fall behind for good. By the end you'll have written a
real program with a game loop, physics, computer riders with their own plans, saved careers, a C library and
races with your friends over the network.
