# Zero to Hero in Zig

Learn to program in Zig from zero by building one game: **Terminal Tour**, a cycling race in your terminal.
You ride, rivals race you, a breakaway goes up the road, the bunch chases, and you sprint for the line.

```text
 TERMINAL TOUR · Ngong Road · 12.5 km                         km 9.8 / 12.5
 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Break   3 riders                     ▶▶▶
  Bunch  42 riders   +0:24      ▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶▶
  You    in the bunch          ▲
 ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Power 285 W  ███████████░░░░   Energy ██████████████░░░   Speed 41 km/h
  9.6 km  The chase is on: Baraka Energy pull at the front
  [↑↓] power  [space] sprint  [s] sit in  [q] quit
```

No programming experience is needed. Each lesson adds one step to the game, and every step is a complete
project you can run, so you can start anywhere and never fall behind for good. By the end you'll have written a
real program with a game loop, physics, computer riders with their own plans, saved careers, a C library and
races with your friends over the network.
